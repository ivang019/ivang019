# Hi, I'm Iván!

I'm an economist based in Mexico City, and I genuinely love working with data. For me, data is the best tool there is for answering real questions, especially when the answer ends up shaping a decision.

## What drives me

- **Good questions first.** I like starting from the decision that needs to be made and working backwards to the data and method that can answer it.
- **Letting the evidence speak.** I test my models, look for where they break, and say clearly what they can and cannot tell us.
- **Always learning.** I keep expanding my toolkit (R is my home base; Python and SQL are growing fast) and I build projects to learn by doing.

## Experience

**Deputy Director of Market Research, Federal Telecommunications Institute (IFT), 2021–2025.** Over four years of individual quantitative studies on market structure, demand and price dynamics, taking each question from data to methodology to written recommendation. This work is internal and confidential, so it isn't published here.

Earlier: data analyst at IMPLAN Guanajuato; economic evaluation of Banobras at El Colegio de México. M.A. in Economics, Development and Public Policy (CIDE); B.A. in Economics (Universidad de Guanajuato).

## Selected work

- **[W26](https://github.com/ivang019/W26):** a probabilistic forecast of the 2026 World Cup (Elo → Poisson → Monte Carlo), backtested against 2018 and 2022. I built it to see how far a simple, honest model can go, and documented where it falls short.
